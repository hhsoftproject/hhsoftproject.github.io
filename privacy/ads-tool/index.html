HHSoft Ads — Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026 · HHSoft

HHSoft Ads is an internal tool operated by HHSoft. It is used only by HHSoft staff to manage HHSoft's own Google Ads accounts, which advertise apps that HHSoft publishes on Google Play. It is not offered to the public and does not access any account that HHSoft does not own.

What the tool accesses

When an HHSoft staff member signs in with Google and grants permission, the tool uses the Google Ads API (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords) to:

  • read campaign settings and performance reports (cost, impressions, installs, conversions) of HHSoft's own Google Ads accounts;
  • create App campaigns in a paused state for human review, and pause campaigns that fail our internal spending rules.

The tool does not request access to Gmail, Drive, Contacts, or any other Google service, and it does not collect personal information about people who see or click our ads.

How the data is stored

The OAuth refresh token and the reports the tool reads are stored only on HHSoft's own computers, in files readable only by the operator account. They are not uploaded to any server operated by HHSoft or by third parties.

Sharing

HHSoft does not sell, rent, or share data obtained through Google APIs with anyone. The data is used only to manage HHSoft's own advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

HHSoft Ads' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored tokens and reports are deleted when the tool is retired.
